Your day, briefed

Starting the day buried in email. Every morning, midday and end of day, Claude reads your inbox and diary and tells you what needs you, what can wait and what's coming up.

Claude knows your business

Explaining yourself again and again. Your team, clients and suppliers, what you do and how you like things written, kept in one place so Claude never needs telling twice.

Paperwork ready to go

Letters, quotes and terms written from scratch every time. Your templates in one place, so Claude drafts the right version for the job and starts a report from the email or call that confirmed what was agreed.

Invoices sent and chased

Awkward to chase, easy to forget. Invoices go out, polite reminders follow when they go overdue, but not if the client has already explained a delay, and a thank-you goes back when you're paid.

Meetings and calls turned into actions

Notes nobody has time to write up. Record a meeting or a phone call with a recorder app or pocket device, and Claude writes the summary, drafts the follow-up email or site notes, and lists what needs doing. If you record a call, tell the other person.

Presentations built for each client

Every pitch starting from a blank slide. Claude builds a presentation that showcases your firm, then tailors it to the client you're pitching to.

Staff expenses, calculated

Expense claims chased at the end of the month. Your team sends in their expenses and Claude adds them up, with a clear total for you to check and approve.
