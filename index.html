Five jobs off your desk, set up in your own email and files

Tap a job to see what it does, with an example of it done.

  1. Every morning, midday and end of day, Claude reads your inbox and diary and tells you what needs you, what can wait and what’s coming up.

  2. Your team, clients and suppliers, what you do and how you like things written, kept in one place so Claude never needs telling twice. I set this up first.

  3. Your letters, quotes and terms in one place, so Claude drafts the right version for the job and starts a report from the email or call that confirmed what was agreed.

  4. Invoices go out, polite reminders follow when they go overdue, but not if the client has already explained a delay, and a thank-you goes back when you’re paid.

  5. Record a meeting or call with a recorder app or pocket device. Claude writes the summary, drafts the follow-up and lists what needs doing. If you record a call, tell the other person.
