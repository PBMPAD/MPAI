What are Claude skills, and why schedule them?

Matthew Price · 09/11/26

A skill is a set of written instructions that teaches Claude to do one job your way, every time. A scheduled task tells Claude to run that skill on its own at set times. Put the two together and Claude stops waiting to be asked. My daily briefs are the clearest example I have.

How I used AI before skills

Before Claude, I used ChatGPT personally, just for asking questions. I'd ask a question and get an answer. That's useful, but it only works when you remember to ask.

What a skill does for me

When I ask for something that matches a skill, Claude picks it up and follows it, so I don't have to explain myself again.

The skills I rely on are built around how I work. Briefs run through the day. File organisation tidies my files and uploads things like site plans and photographs. None of them would suit another business exactly. They're built for mine.

My briefs: from sometimes to every day

I have three briefs, for the morning, midday and end of day. Each one reads my inbox and calendar and tells me where things stand.

When I first built them, I ran them when I remembered. Putting them on a schedule changed that. Now they arrive on their own, and I:

  • start the day knowing what's urgent
  • let less slip through
  • spend less time checking my inbox
  • plan the rest of the week better

What changes when you schedule it?

A scheduled task is a job Claude runs at a set time without you asking. For me, that was the step from using Claude as a chatbot to having it work as an assistant. It's also how I set up the briefs for MPAI clients.

Where this leads

Once a few skills run on a schedule, the gaps in your day start to close. That's the idea behind No Gap Working, which I cover in my next post. If you'd like to see what skills could do for your business, get in touch.
