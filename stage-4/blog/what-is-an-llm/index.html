What is an LLM? Copilot, ChatGPT and Claude, and why I chose Claude

Matthew Price · 08/11/26

An LLM, or large language model, is the technology behind AI tools like Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT and Claude. I've used all three in different parts of my life. Claude is the one I use for work, in my day job and to build automations for small businesses. The reason is that it lets me build skills and processes that are unique to my business and yours.

What is an LLM?

LLM stands for large language model. It has learned from an enormous amount of writing, so it can read what you type and write a reply, word by word. That's how it can draft an email or summarise a long document. It can also sound confident and still be wrong, so anything that matters still needs checking.

Microsoft Copilot

Copilot is Microsoft's AI, built into Word, Outlook, Excel and Teams. I have it at work through Microsoft 365, so it's there in the apps I use every day. It isn't the one I chose to build on, though, for reasons I'll come to.

ChatGPT

ChatGPT, made by OpenAI, is the most used AI assistant in the world, with more than a billion people using it every month. I knew ChatGPT before I knew Claude, but I only ever used it personally, for asking questions. I never used it for work.

Claude

Claude is made by Anthropic. I started with it where most people start, sorting emails in simple batches. Then I moved on to documents, and then to trackers, which are running lists that Claude keeps up to date for me. I built plenty and scrapped most. What I ended up with is a set of skills and processes that are unique to how I work.

How I use Claude in my day job

In my day job, I'm an associate director and chartered surveyor at a busy firm. Claude helps me draft and check reports, pull the key points out of long documents, and write emails and letters that sound like me.

The bigger change is the skills. I have briefs that run through the day and tell me what's urgent, so less slips through and I spend less time checking my inbox. I also have Claude organise and tidy my files, and upload things like site plans and photographs.

None of that came ready-made. I built each one around the way I work.

Why I build MPAI on Claude

MPAI sets up Claude for small businesses so owners spend less time on admin. I chose Claude for four reasons.

  • Skills and scheduled tasks. I can teach Claude a job once and have it run on its own at set times. Once a job runs without me asking, it's a real automation.
  • It works with the apps you already use. Claude connects to Outlook, calendars and files, so it works from your real emails and diary.
  • It's easy for owners to use. When I've rolled out processes to staff, anything that wasn't extremely easy to use didn't get taken up. If you can send a message, you can use this.
  • It's careful. The way I set it up, Claude drafts and then asks before anything is sent.
