Tell me about your businessYour business

Hello, I’m Matthew. A chartered surveyor, not a tech company.

I work in residential surveying in London. I started using AI to take the repetitive work off my desk: drafting replies, chasing people, turning site notes into reports, keeping clients updated.

It worked well enough that I now help other small businesses do the same. I know what it's like to run a busy inbox alongside the real work, so I keep things simple: your jobs, your tools, plain English.

I live in Brentwood, Essex, with my young family. Away from work I'm usually on a golf course trying to get back to the 6 handicap of my youth, following Watford, or worrying about my fantasy football team in a league with my mates from back home in Leighton Buzzard.

What it changed in my own day

  • Whole days backDays carved out of my workload, now spent on planning, outreach and networking.
  • Same-day repliesEmails that used to wait until the next day now go the same day.
  • Nothing slipsFollow-ups and chasers no longer get forgotten.
  • Drafts, not blank pagesQuotes and reports start from a draft that’s already most of the way there.
  • Fewer chasersClients chase me far less than they used to.
