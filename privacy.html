Privacy policy

Last updated: 09/11/26

This policy explains what personal information MPAI collects, why, how long it is kept and what your rights are. If anything is unclear, email matthew@mpai.site.

Who we are

MPAI is run by Matthew Price as a sole trader, working from home in Brentwood, Essex. MPAI sets up Claude, an AI assistant, for small businesses so owners spend less time on admin.

MPAI decides how your personal information is used, which makes MPAI the “controller” under UK data protection law. MPAI is registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). Registration number: TBC.

What this policy covers

  • Visiting mpai.site
  • Sending an enquiry through the website
  • Emails, calls and meetings with MPAI
  • Being an MPAI client

What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, business name, website, the jobs you pick and anything you write to us.
  • Emails and messages: what you send us and our replies.
  • Calls and meetings: audio recordings, written transcripts, and the summaries and action points made from them (see below).
  • Client work: the details we need to set up and support Claude for your business.
  • Accounts: invoices and payment records. We never see or keep card numbers.
  • Website visits: our hosting provider keeps basic technical logs, such as your IP address, to keep the site running and secure. We do not use analytics or tracking cookies.

Recording calls and meetings

We record some phone calls and meetings with a Pocket recorder, so we have an accurate record of what was agreed and nothing gets missed.

  • This policy is our notice that calls and meetings may be recorded, so we do not announce it at the start of every call. If you would rather a call or meeting was not recorded, just tell us and we will not record it.
  • The recording is turned into a written transcript. We then use Claude to write a short summary, list the action points and draft any follow-up emails.
  • Matthew checks every summary and email before it is used. Nothing is sent automatically, and no decisions about you are made by a computer alone.
  • Recordings and transcripts are deleted within 30 days. The summary and action points are kept with your enquiry or client record for the periods below.

Why we use your information

UK law says we need a lawful reason for each use. Ours are:

  • Replying to your enquiry and preparing a quote: because you asked us to, before any contract.
  • Doing the work you have agreed to: to carry out our contract with you.
  • Recording calls and meetings: our legitimate interest in keeping an accurate record and following up properly. You can object at any time.
  • Keeping accounts and records: because the law requires it.

We do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Your business’s own data

When we set Claude up for your business, we may see information about your customers, staff or suppliers, for example in your inbox. We only use it to do the work you have asked for, under our agreement with you, and we do not keep it for any other purpose.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We only share it with the services that help us run MPAI, who act on our instructions:

  • Microsoft 365: email and calendar
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting
  • Google Fonts: the fonts on this website, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads
  • Netlify: domain name management
  • Formspree: the website enquiry forms
  • Pocket (Open Vision Engineering Inc.): call and meeting recording and transcription
  • Anthropic (Claude): writing summaries and draft emails

We will also share information if the law requires it.

Information sent outside the UK

Several of these services are based in the United States, so your information may be stored or handled there. When this happens we rely on safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK-US data bridge or approved contract terms.

How long we keep it

  • Call and meeting recordings and transcripts: up to 30 days
  • Enquiries that do not go ahead: 12 months from your last contact
  • Client records: for as long as we work together, then 6 years, in case of questions or claims
  • Invoices and accounts: as long as HMRC requires, usually at least 5 years after the tax return deadline

After that, we delete it.

Keeping it safe

We use services with strong security, protect our accounts with passwords and two-step sign-in, and only keep what we need.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your information
  • stop or limit how we use it, including asking us not to record calls
  • send your information to you or someone else in a common format

Email matthew@mpai.site. It is free, and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if anything changes and show the new date at the top.
