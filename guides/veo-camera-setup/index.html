How to set up a Veo camera to record a match

Updated 28/11/26 · for Veo Cam 3 (Veo Cam 2 works the same way)

A Veo camera sits on a tall tripod at the side of the pitch and films the whole game on its own. Afterwards it uploads the match, follows the ball automatically and picks out the goals and big moments. Nobody has to stand there filming. This guide covers what to do the night before, at the ground and once you get home, with a few short videos if you'd rather watch than read.

The night before

  1. Charge the camera fully. Give it at least 4 hours on charge. A flat camera is the most common reason a match doesn't get recorded.
  2. Install the Veo Cam app on your phone and sign in with your club's Veo account.
  3. Check there's space on the camera. Connect to it in the app and open Settings to see the free storage. Older matches stay on the camera until they've uploaded.
  4. Pack the tripod. Make sure the quick mount (the plate the camera clips on to) is with it.

At the ground

  1. Pick your spot. Stand level with the halfway line, 3 to 5 metres back from the touchline. Keep the sun or floodlights behind the camera, not facing it.
  2. Clip the camera on and switch it on. Attach it firmly to the tripod's quick mount.
  3. Raise the tripod to full height. The higher it is, the better the camera sees the far side of the pitch.
  4. Check the picture in the app. Connect to the camera and make sure you can see all four corners of the pitch.
  5. Press Record. You can add the match details (teams, kick-off time) as you go.
  6. Leave it alone. Don't move or knock the tripod once it's recording. It throws off the ball tracking and the highlights.

At full time, stop the recording in the app, lower the tripod and pack the camera away.

Once you're home

  1. Connect the camera to Wi-Fi. It uploads the match on its own. You can watch progress in the app under Library, On camera.
  2. Give it time. A 90-minute match can take 1.5 to 2 hours to upload on a normal home connection. Leaving it overnight is fine.
  3. Wait for the edit. Once uploaded, the version that follows the ball is usually ready within about an hour, with highlight clips a few minutes after that.
  4. Watch and share. The match appears in your club's Veo Clubhouse, where you can watch it, clip moments and share links with parents and players.
