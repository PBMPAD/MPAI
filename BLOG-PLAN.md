# Blog plan: one post per skill, every 3 days

Agreed 17/11/26. Each post is 500 to 800 words, written with the mpai-blog-writer skill and checked with the anti-ai-writing skill.

## How it works

1. Two days before each publish date, Claude drafts the post from how the skill works (Matthew's own method), leaving [your example: ...] gaps for his experience, and asks a few pop-up questions.
2. Matthew answers. Claude finishes the post, runs the anti-ai-writing check again and shows the final text.
3. Only after Matthew approves: Claude builds `blog/<slug>/index.html`, adds the card to the top of `blog/index.html`, and asks before anything goes live.
4. Nothing is published, uploaded or sent without Matthew saying yes.

Rules for every post: at least 80% original (Matthew's own experience, methods and opinions). No prices unless Matthew gives them and they match the pricing page. No examples from professional services or surveying. Never name his employer.

Status: planned, drafted, approved, live.
