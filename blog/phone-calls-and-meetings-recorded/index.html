How do you stop phone calls slipping through the gaps?

Matthew Price · 11/11/26

Record the call, let Claude turn it into notes and a draft follow-up, then check it and send it yourself.

What goes wrong after a phone call

You are a busy business owner. A call ends and you have agreed a price, a date and who is doing what. Ten minutes later you are on the next one.

Four things tend to happen afterwards.

  • You forget details. Names, figures and dates blur within a day.
  • You write your notes from memory, and they are never as good as the call.
  • The follow-up email does not get sent, because nothing prompts you to send it.
  • Weeks later someone says "that is not what we agreed", and you cannot show what was said.

App or device: which do you need?

You can record calls with an app on your phone, or with a small separate recorder. I have used a recording app a lot over the past year, and I have recently added a small recorder as well. The app does not work on every phone, so it depends what you have. The recorder clips onto the back of your phone, so it does not have that problem. Either way, you need a transcript Claude can read. The recording was never the slow part. Getting from a recording to something I could use was.

What I do with the recording

Once the recording has a transcript, I ask Claude to read it. It gives me:

  • a short plain-English summary
  • the action points and who owns each
  • a draft follow-up email, saved in Outlook as a draft
  • a file note I can keep with the client or job

I read each one and press send myself. Nothing goes out otherwise.

Do you need permission to record?

Recording rules vary, so check the ones that apply to you. For my own calls, the disclosure is in the privacy policy on my website, so I do not have to ask "can I record this call?" every time. Anyone who would rather not be recorded only has to say. Recordings and transcripts are deleted within 30 days.

Meetings work the same way

Put your phone or recorder on the table and press record. Afterwards you get the same summary, action points, draft emails and file note.
