Who it’s for

Owner-run businesses where the owner answers the emails and sends the quotes as well as doing the work: trades, cafés, shops and other small local businesses. You don’t need any AI experience. If you can write an email, you can use this.

The jobs it does for you

  • Your day, briefed. A short note each morning on what needs you, what can wait and what’s coming up.
  • Your business, on file. Your team, customers and suppliers kept in one place, so nothing needs explaining twice.
  • Paperwork ready to go. Quotes, letters and terms drafted from your own versions.
  • Invoices sent and chased. Invoices go out, polite reminders follow, and a thank-you when you’re paid.
  • Meetings and calls turned into actions. A summary, follow-up email and list of actions from each recorded call.
