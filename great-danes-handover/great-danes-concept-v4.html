Join Great Danes

Four steps, from finding the team to the first match.

  1. 1

    Find your child's age group

    Age groups go by age on 31 August, which matches the school year. Pick your child's school year to see the teams, training times and places.

  2. 2

    Come and try it

    Pups (Reception and Year 1): just come along on Saturday at 09:00. It's £5 a session, paid on the day.

    Wildcats (girls, Years 2 and 3): Saturdays 08:45 to 10:00.

    Other teams: email the Registration Secretary, Andrew Mitchell, with your child's name, school year and any experience. He'll arrange a trial session with the manager.

  3. 3

    Create an account and apply

    Membership applications are online only. Create an account on the club site, then apply for membership and pay on the [Membership and payments page].

    You'll need:

    • your child's date of birth and school year
    • a parent's contact details
    • a decision on photo consent
    • any medical needs, which go privately to the manager, never through Eva
  4. 4

    Confirm the FA registration

    For league teams, the club registers your child with the FA. You'll get an email asking you to confirm it. Please reply, because your child can't play matches until you do.
