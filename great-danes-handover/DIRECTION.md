# Great Danes concept v4: surface brief

Prepared 21/11/26 with Impeccable 4.10.0 (new-work playbook, run by hand: the `impeccable` launcher was blocked in this environment, so concept-seed, first-viewport and detect did not run).

Scope: concept site of four pages (home, Join, Help with Eva, team page template). Mode: Operate, with a Persuade moment on joining. Audience: parents, mostly on a phone, at the pitch side on a weekend morning or at home in the evening; then existing members, volunteers and sponsors. Job: answer "can my child play here, when, where, how much, who do I ask" without a message to a volunteer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the kit. Every age group is a red shirt number on a white shirt, and the visitor finds their child's number by school year. Refuses the category default of a stock-photo hero with news cards and a 13-item menu.

OWN-WORLD: White shirt ground, black panel bands (header, opening section, footer) like the shirt's shoulder panels, crest red only for numerals, the main action and links. Archivo, wide and heavy for numerals and headings, normal width for text. Hairline rules, square corners, no cards, no shadows except the photo.

STORY: A parent sees their own club's players celebrating, picks a school year, sees the team, day, time and cost, and taps Join. A member asks Eva or opens a help heading and gets an answer with a named owner.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: crest bar, five links, then the team photo full width, then on black the headline "Football for girls and boys from Reception to Year 13" with "Find your child's team" (school-year picker) and "Join the club". Desktop: black band, headline and actions left, photo right at native size.

FORM: Kit and squad numbers, first of seven listed (kit, matchday programme, clubhouse noticeboard, FA Full-Time tables, line paint, school newsletter, team sheet). seed: none (unavailable). Direction chosen by the user on 21/11/26: "Revisit with photo", bolder and photographic like Brighton.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
