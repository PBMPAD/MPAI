How it works, in plain steps

From your first message to a working set-up.

The steps

  1. Tick your jobs

    Pick your type of business and tick the jobs that eat your week, plus up to two of your own. I’ll reply by email to book a time.

  2. An hour together

    Face to face at your place of work, or a Teams call if you’re short on time. I show you a mock set-up of your business, built around the jobs you picked, and we talk each one through in detail.

  3. I build your set-up

    I go away and build it for your own email, calendar and files, whether you use Microsoft 365 or Google. It starts with your business, team and clients, so everything sounds like you. Simple chasers go out on their own; anything important waits for your OK.

  4. Sent to you, step by step

    Your set-up arrives with step-by-step instructions, so you can get it running on your own computer.

  5. An hour of support

    Up to another hour of remote support to make sure it’s all working, including any tweaks.

  6. Then care plans or bigger builds

    After that, an optional care plan keeps it running and adds new jobs each month, or I can quote for a larger bespoke build. See the care plans

What it costs, in one line

Set-up is from £1,000 for all five jobs. Care plans from £100 a month are optional. Your Claude Pro plan is separate, about £18 a month.

Questions people ask

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. If you can write an email, you can use this. You ask for things in plain English.

Will it send emails without me seeing them?

Only the simple, low-risk ones, such as invites and invoice chasers. Anything higher-risk, AI drafts and you approve before it goes.

What do I need to pay for?

Your own Claude Pro plan, paid to Anthropic: about £18 a month, or less if you pay yearly. Set-up is from £1,000 and covers all five jobs. Any extra jobs of your own are quoted once I've seen them. After that, a care plan is optional: Maintenance is £100 a month, Growth is £250 a month, and bigger projects are priced on request.

Microsoft or Google?

Either. It connects to Microsoft 365 work accounts and to Google accounts.

Where does my data go?

Your emails and files stay in your own accounts. AI reads them only when you ask it to do something.

Got two more jobs of your own?

Send me two jobs that aren’t on my list and I’ll tell you if I can take them off your desk too.
